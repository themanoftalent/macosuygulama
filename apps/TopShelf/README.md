# TopShelf — Folders in Menu Bar

A lightweight, native macOS menu bar app that lets you pin folders to your menu bar for one-click browsing of subfolders and files, dragging files directly into apps and browsers, Quick Look hover previews, and customizable folder icons.

## Features

- **Multiple Pinned Folders**: Pin any number of folders to the macOS menu bar and reorder them freely.
- **One-Click & Cascading Subfolder Browsing**: Click a menu bar icon to browse files with native macOS icons; hover or click subfolders (`>`) to expand cascading menus.
- **Native Drag & Drop**: Drag files directly out of the menu bar into browsers (upload zones), Mail, Slack, Finder, or any macOS app — or drop files onto a folder panel or menu bar icon to copy them in.
- **Floating Quick Look Preview**: Hover over files to preview PDFs, images, videos, and documents beside the menu.
- **Flexible Sorting**: Per-folder sorting by Name, Date Modified, Date Added, Size, or Kind (Ascending/Descending, Folders on Top, Show Hidden Files).
- **Icon Customization**: Choose from 24 SF Symbols, 10 color tints (or monochrome template), and optional menu bar title labels.
- **100% Local & Open Source**: Runs completely locally on your Mac with zero telemetry.

## Build from Source

```bash
./build.sh
```

This compiles the release Swift binary, generates `AppIcon.icns`, assembles `build/TopShelf.app`, and ad-hoc signs it.
